Data is fetched on request. Nothing is bundled with the plugin, and results are cached on the device.

MusicBrainz  https://musicbrainz.org/ws/2/
  Artist and release-group search, lookup and browse. Lookups request genres and ratings.
  Core data (names, titles, dates, IDs, artist credits) is CC0 (public domain).
  Genres and ratings shown in Discover are supplementary data, (c) MusicBrainz contributors,
  licensed CC BY-NC-SA 3.0: https://musicbrainz.org/doc/About/Data_License

ListenBrainz
  https://api.listenbrainz.org/1/explore/fresh-releases/  (new and upcoming releases)
  https://labs.api.listenbrainz.org/similar-artists/json  (similar artists)
  ListenBrainz listen data and datasets are released under CC0: https://listenbrainz.org/data/

Privacy: artist names from your library, and any text you search for, are sent to MusicBrainz.
