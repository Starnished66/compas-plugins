Rendered from the 22px symbolic SVGs and filled with the player's native
submenu gradient (gold, cyan, mint, coral or purple), at each file's
original size.

Mapping (icons/<path>):
  discover.png       <- actions/22/compass-symbolic.svg (purple)
  owned.png          <- actions/22/checkmark-symbolic.svg (mint)
  wish.png           <- actions/22/love-symbolic.svg (coral)
  owned_wish.png     <- status/22/starred-symbolic.svg (gold)
